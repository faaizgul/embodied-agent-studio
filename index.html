Design questions for the body

Realistic or stylised? The closer an avatar gets to human without quite matching, the more uneasy people can feel. Does its look match its job and the people it serves? What does its posture say before it speaks?

The brain is a large language model plus your character sheet. Everything you write below becomes the instructions the model reads before every reply.

Character sheet
Paste facts, FAQs or rules. Keep it under about 2 pages. Never paste personal data: free tiers may use what you send to improve their models.

          Design questions for the voice
          
Does the voice match the body? What accent and pace suit the people it serves? Should it sound human at all, or clearly synthetic? How does it signal that it is listening, or thinking?
